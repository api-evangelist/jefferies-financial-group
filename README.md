# Jefferies Financial Group (jefferies-financial-group)
Jefferies Financial Group Inc. is a diversified financial services company engaged in investment banking and capital markets, asset management, and direct investing. Operating through its principal subsidiary Jefferies LLC, the firm serves institutional investors, corporations, and governments worldwide with research, trading, advisory, and underwriting services.
