While prediction markets remain hard to allocate to directly, their clearest near-term use case for hedge funds may be informational rather than transactional. Prediction-market prices can offer continuously updated, market-implied probabilities for events such as elections, rate decisions, litigation outcomes, regulatory approvals, economic releases and corporate catalysts.
