Most CFOs have invested heavily in the technology that governs how their companies spend money. The software governing everything that happens after a deal is signed, how they invoice and collect, until recently, has been patchwork: held together with spreadsheets, professional services, and institutional patience. Rebecca Schwartz, Co-Founder of Tabs, is building the platform she believes will finally change that.
